# Demo Day Script

This script is designed for a 60-90 second presentation.

## Preparation
1. Open the Memento app on your phone or laptop.
2. Have 3-5 photos ready (family, travel, etc.).
3. Ensure you are in a quiet environment for the voice interview.

## 60-Second Script

- **[0:00] Intro:** "This is Memento. We're using Amazon Nova AI to help people preserve their life stories through simple voice conversations."
- **[0:05] Upload:** "I'll start by uploading a few photos from my summer vacation and entering my email." *(Upload photos & click Start)*
- **[0:15] Interview:** "The AI biographer is now ready to talk. It's using Nova Sonic to listen and respond in real-time."
- **[0:20] Memory:** *(Hold to Respond)* "This photo was taken at the beach last July. My kids built their first sandcastle, and we stayed until the sun went down."
- **[0:35] AI Follow-up:** "Notice how the AI asks relevant follow-up questions, making the storytelling feel natural."
- **[0:45] Finish:** "I'm done sharing. I'll click 'Finish My Story'. Now, Nova Lite is editing the transcript into a professional biography, while Nova Embeddings matches the best photos to the text."
- **[0:55] Result:** "And here it is. A beautifully formatted PDF biography, ready to be shared with family, already sent to my inbox."

## Key Selling Points for Judges
- **Nova Integration:** Using Sonic (Voice), Lite (Text), and Embeddings (Multimodal).
- **Zero Friction:** No typing required; preserve memories just by talking.
- **Tangible Output:** Produces a high-quality, permanent record (PDF).
- **Serverless:** Scalable and cost-effective architecture.
